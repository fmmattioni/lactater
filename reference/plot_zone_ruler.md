# The zones as a ruler

One bar of the zones on the intensity axis, and under each boundary the
intensity, pace, heart rate and RPE where one zone ends and the next
begins (those the zones have). For internal use only.

## Usage

``` r
plot_zone_ruler(object, zones, limits, colours)
```

## Arguments

- object:

  The result of
  [`lactate_zones()`](https://fmmattioni.github.io/lactater/reference/lactate_zones.md).

- zones:

  Its `lactater_zones` attribute.

- limits:

  The intensity range of the plot.

- colours:

  The plot colours.
