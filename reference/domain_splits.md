# The two splits inside the moderate and the severe domain (five-zone model)

The two splits inside the moderate and the severe domain (five-zone
model)

## Usage

``` r
domain_splits(split, lt, info)
```

## Arguments

- split:

  `"relative"`, `"seiler"`, or two intensities.

- lt:

  LT1 and LT2.

- info:

  The `lactater` attribute of the results.

## Value

the zone 1\|2 and zone 4\|5 intensities.
