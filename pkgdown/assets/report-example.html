LT1 at 111 W, LT2 at 140 W

A cycling step test of 7 stages, up to 191 W. LT1 (LTP1) and LT2 (ModDmax) split the intensities into three domains: below LT1 exercise is moderate, between them heavy, and above LT2 severe, where lactate keeps rising even at a constant intensity.
